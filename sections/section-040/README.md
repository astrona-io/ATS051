# Alerting basics (when, what, and why)

This section covers when to alert, what to alert on and why. Module 1 explains actionable alerts, symptoms and error budgets. Module 2 turns a service level objective into burn-rate alerts and shows how to test that the alerting pipeline works.

Read each module's goals, parts and summary in order. Labs follow the part they practise. The section ends with a capstone lab and a knowledge check.

## Learning Outcomes

- Define an alert's owner, impact and expected action.
- Distinguish urgent symptoms from diagnostic causes.
- Relate SLO error budgets and burn rates to response timing.
- Write a multi-window burn-rate alert with severity and runbook metadata.
- Evaluate low traffic, maintenance and monitoring failures, and test the alerting pipeline.
