# Dashboards That Explain Population, Units And Gaps

This module develops dashboards That Explain Population, Units And Gaps. Use the explanations to connect intended behavior with responsible components and observable evidence.

## Learning Goals

- Choose panels for rate, duration, errors and saturation.
- Explain time range, resolution and units.
- Preserve grouping and denominator context.
- Distinguish display gaps from measured zero and alert evaluation.

## Before You Start

Examples illustrate declared relationships and reasoning. No execution environment is required or claimed.
