# Actionable Alerts, Symptoms And Error-Budget Reasoning

This module develops actionable Alerts, Symptoms And Error-Budget Reasoning. Use the explanations to connect intended behavior with responsible components and observable evidence.

## Learning Goals

- Define an alert's owner, impact and expected action.
- Distinguish urgent symptoms from diagnostic causes.
- Relate SLO error budgets and burn rates to response timing.
- Evaluate low traffic, maintenance and monitoring failures.

## Before You Start

Examples illustrate declared relationships and reasoning. No execution environment is required or claimed.
