# Alerting basics (when, what, and why)

This section covers alerting basics (when, what, and why). It develops actionable Alerts, Symptoms And Error-Budget Reasoning through mechanisms, examples, prerequisites and failure reasoning.

Read the module goals, teaching and closing summary in order. YAML checks are indexed separately and appear after designated summaries. Larger domains include an intermediate checkpoint before the final review.

## Learning Outcomes

- Define an alert's owner, impact and expected action.
- Distinguish urgent symptoms from diagnostic causes.
- Relate SLO error budgets and burn rates to response timing.
- Evaluate low traffic, maintenance and monitoring failures.
